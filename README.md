# Project Overview

BitForgeNano is an innovative, open-source Bitcoin miner designed for home use. Our goal is to democratize Bitcoin mining by providing an efficient, accessible solution for individual miners.

## Key Features

1.  **Powerful Performance**: Utilizing 2 of the BM1368 ASIC chip, BitForgeNano aims to deliver over 1 TH/s (Terahash per second) of mining power.
    
2.  **Open Source**: Our commitment to transparency and community involvement means all aspects of BitForgeNano's design and software are open source.
    
3.  **Home-Friendly**: Specifically engineered for home use, balancing power and efficiency for the average enthusiast.
    
4.  **Ongoing Development**: While we're targeting 1+ TH/s, our prototype suggests even higher performance potential.
    

## Why BitForgeNano Matters

*   **Decentralization**: By enabling more individuals to participate in mining, we strengthen Bitcoin's network security and decentralization.
    
*   **Accessibility**: Lower the barrier to entry for Bitcoin mining, making it feasible for tech enthusiasts and crypto supporters to contribute directly to the network.
    
*   **Innovation**: Our open-source approach encourages community-driven improvements and adaptations.
    

## Current Status

BitForgeNano is in active development.
